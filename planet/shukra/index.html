Shukra (Venus)

शुक्र देव
Ruling Deity: Goddess Lakshmi / Durga 🗓 Auspicious Day: Friday
Why This Deity?

The Cosmic Connection

Goddess Lakshmi governs wealth, beauty, luxury, and harmonious relationships, perfectly mirroring Venus.

The Sacred Mantra
Sanskrit
ॐ द्रां द्रीं द्रौं सः शुक्राय नमः
Hindi
ओम द्रां द्रीं द्रौं सः शुक्राय नमः
English Transliteration
Om Draam Dreem Droum Sah Shukraya Namah
Recommended Bhajans

Sacred Songs for Shukra (Venus)

  • Mahalakshmi Ashtakam
  • Sri Suktam
  • Ya Devi Sarvabhuteshu
Astrological Guidance

🕐When to Chant

Chant on Fridays, during Shukra Mahadasha, or to attract abundance and marital happiness. Offer white flowers, kheer (rice pudding), and perfume to Goddess Lakshmi. Venus governs love, art, and luxury—those facing relationship challenges or financial stagnation find great relief in this mantra chanted 108 times every Friday.

🙏 Presiding Deity: Lord Hanuman

What is Angarak Dosha?

Angarak Dosha is formed when Mars and Rahu or Ketu are conjunct in the birth chart. It causes an excess of fire energy, leading to aggression, accidents, and disputes.

What is Angarak Dosha?
Angarak Dosha tends to occur when Mars and Rahu are conjunct in the birth chart. Both are fiery and intensifying energies — their combination may amplify aggression, impulsiveness, or sudden events.
Is Angarak Dosha serious?
Its intensity depends significantly on the house it occupies, the signs involved, and moderating aspects from Jupiter or other benefics. Many people with this combination live normal, balanced lives.
What are typical effects?
Heightened temper, risk-taking behaviour, accidents-prone periods, or explosive professional situations may be associated. These tend to intensify during Mars or Rahu dashas.
Who is the presiding deity?
Lord Hanuman is the primary deity for Mars-related doshas. Rahu's energy is traditionally addressed through Durga worship and Navagraha prayers.
What is the traditional remedy?
Hanuman Chalisa daily, Tuesday and Saturday Hanuman temple visits, and offering red sindoor are traditional. Avoiding impulsive decisions and cultivating patience is equally recommended.
Does house placement matter?
Significantly. Angarak in the 5th or 9th may affect children or dharma; in the 7th may affect relationships; in the 10th may create workplace conflicts. Each requires chart-specific interpretation.
Can this combination be positive?
Often yes — in certain charts it may create extraordinary drive, competitive success, or breakthroughs in high-pressure environments. Rahu amplifies Mars' ambition.
How does Jupiter help?
Jupiter's aspect on the conjunction tends to be one of the most effective moderating influences — it may bring wisdom and restraint to an otherwise volatile combination.

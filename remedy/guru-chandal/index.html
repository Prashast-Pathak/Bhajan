Guru Chandal Dosha
🌸 Presiding Deity: Lord Vishnu
📖
What is Guru Chandal Dosha?

Guru Chandal Dosha forms when Jupiter is conjunct or aspected by Rahu. It corrupts one's wisdom, ethics, and can lead a person toward false teachers or immoral paths.

🎵
Primary Mantra / Bhajan
🌸
Recommended Daily Practice
Vishnu Sahasranama
Dedicated to Lord Vishnu · Recite with sincere devotion
🪴
Step-by-Step Remedial Actions
1
Chant Vishnu Sahasranama every Thursday
2
Donate yellow items like turmeric and yellow cloth
3
Wear a yellow sapphire (Pukhraj) after consulting an astrologer
4
Fast on Thursdays and offer prayers to Lord Vishnu
⚠ Severity Notice
Severity: Moderate to High
Important Guidance for Guru Chandal Dosha

This dosha represents an area of karmic challenge. However, sincere spiritual practice, awareness, and remedial actions can significantly mitigate its negative effects.

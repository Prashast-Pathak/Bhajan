Bhagavad Gita 16.7

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च जना न विदुरासुराः।न शौचं नापि चाचारो न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते।।16.7।।

Meaning: The demoniacal men neither know action nor renunciation. They have no cleanliness, nor right conduct, nor truth.

Bhagavad Gita 18.40

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: न तदस्ति पृथिव्यां वा दिवि देवेषु वा पुनः।सत्त्वं प्रकृतिजैर्मुक्तं यदेभिः स्यात्ित्रभिर्गुणैः।।18.40।।

Meaning: There is no creature, on earth or among the gods in heaven, that is exempt from these three Gunas born of Prakrti.

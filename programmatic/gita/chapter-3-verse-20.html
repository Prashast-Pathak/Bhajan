Bhagavad Gita 3.20

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: कर्मणैव हि संसिद्धिमास्थिता जनकादयः। लोकसंग्रहमेवापि सम्पश्यन्कर्तुमर्हसि॥

Meaning: Kings such as Janaka attained perfection solely by performance of prescribed duties. Therefore, just for the sake of educating the people in general (lokasamgraha), you should perform your work.

Bhagavad Gita 8.5

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम्। यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः।।8.5।।

Meaning: And at the time of death, anyone who departs, giving up the body while thinking of Me alone, attains My state; there is no doubt about this.

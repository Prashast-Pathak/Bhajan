Bhagavad Gita 11.28

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: यथा नदीनां बहवोऽम्बुवेगाः समुद्रमेवाभिमुखाः द्रवन्ति। तथा तवामी नरलोकवीरा विशन्ति वक्त्राण्यभिविज्वलन्ति।।11.28।।

Meaning: As many torrents of rivers flow towards the ocean, so do these heroes of the world of men enter into Your flaming mouths.

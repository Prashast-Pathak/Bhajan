Bhagavad Gita 13.31

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: यदा भूतपृथग्भावमेकस्थमनुपश्यति।तत एव च विस्तारं ब्रह्म सम्पद्यते तदा।।13.31।।

Meaning: When he perceives the independent modes of existence of all beings centered in one, and their expansion from it alone, then he attains Brahman.

Bhagavad Gita 12.8

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय।निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः।।12.8।।

Meaning: Focus your mind on Me alone; and let your intellect enter into Me. Then, you will live in Me alone; there is no doubt.

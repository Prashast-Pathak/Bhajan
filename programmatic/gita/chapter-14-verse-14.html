Bhagavad Gita 14.14

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: यदा सत्त्वे प्रवृद्धे तु प्रलयं याति देहभृत्।तदोत्तमविदां लोकानमलान्प्रतिपद्यते।।14.14।।

Meaning: If the embodied self dissolves when Sattva prevails, then it proceeds to the pure worlds of those who know the highest.

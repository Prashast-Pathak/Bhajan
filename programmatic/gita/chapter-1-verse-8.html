Bhagavad Gita 1.8

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: भवान्भीष्मश्च कर्णश्च कृपश्च समितिञ्जयः। अश्वत्थामा विकर्णश्च सौमदत्तिस्तथैव च॥

Meaning: There are personalities like yourself, Bhishma, Karna, Kripa, who are always victorious in battle. There are also Ashvatthama, Vikarna, and the son of Somadatta called Bhurishrava.

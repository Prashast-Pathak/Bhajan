Bhagavad Gita 1.2

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: सञ्जय उवाच दृष्ट्वा तु पाण्डवानीकं व्यूढं दुर्योधनस्तदा। आचार्यमुपसङ्गम्य राजा वचनमब्रवीत्॥

Meaning: Sanjaya said: O King, after looking over the army arrayed in military formation by the sons of Pandu, King Duryodhana went to his teacher and spoke the following words.

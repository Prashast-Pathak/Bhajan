Bhagavad Gita 18.66

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज।अहं त्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा शुचः।।18.66।।

Meaning: Relinquishing all Dharmas completely, seek Me alone for refuge. I will release you from all sins; grieve not.

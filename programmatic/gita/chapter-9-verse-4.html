Bhagavad Gita 9.4

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना। मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः।।9.4।।

Meaning: This entire universe is pervaded by Me, in an unmanifest form. All beings abide in Me, but I do not abide in them.

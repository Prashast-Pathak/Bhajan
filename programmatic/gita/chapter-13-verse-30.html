Bhagavad Gita 13.30

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: प्रकृत्यैव च कर्माणि क्रियमाणानि सर्वशः।यः पश्यति तथाऽऽत्मानमकर्तारं स पश्यति।।13.30।।

Meaning: He who sees that all acts are done universally by Prakrti alone, and likewise that the self is not the doer, he indeed sees.

Bhagavad Gita 1.1

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: धृतराष्ट्र उवाच धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः। मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय॥

Meaning: Dhritarashtra said: O Sanjaya, what did my sons and the sons of Pandu do, having assembled at the holy land of Kurukshetra, eager to fight?

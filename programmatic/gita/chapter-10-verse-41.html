Bhagavad Gita 10.41

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा। तत्तदेवावगच्छ त्वं मम तेजोंऽशसंभवम्।।10.41।।

Meaning: Whatever being is possessed of power, or splendour, or energy, know that as coming from a fragment of My power.

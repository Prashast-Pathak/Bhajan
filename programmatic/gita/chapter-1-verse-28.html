Bhagavad Gita 1.28

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: कृपया परयाविष्टो विषीदन्निदमब्रवीत्। अर्जुन उवाच दृष्ट्वेमं स्वजनं कृष्ण युयुत्सुं समुपस्थितम्॥

Meaning: ...he was overwhelmed with compassion and spoke thus in deep sorrow. Arjuna said: O Krishna, seeing my friends and relatives present before me in such a fighting spirit...

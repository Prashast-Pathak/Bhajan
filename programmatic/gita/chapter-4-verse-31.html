Bhagavad Gita 4.31

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: यज्ञशिष्टामृतभुजो यान्ति ब्रह्म सनातनम्। नायं लोकोऽस्त्ययज्ञस्य कुतोऽन्यः कुरुसत्तम॥

Meaning: O best of the Kurus, without sacrifice one can never live happily on this planet or in this life: what then of the next? Those who taste the nectar of the remnants of sacrifice go to the supreme eternal atmosphere.

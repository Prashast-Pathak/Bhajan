Bhagavad Gita 5.1

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: अर्जुन उवाच सन्न्यासं कर्मणां कृष्ण पुनर्योगं च शंससि। यच्छ्रेय एतयोरेकं तन्मे ब्रूहि सुनिश्चितम्॥

Meaning: Arjuna said: O Krishna, first of all You ask me to renounce work, and then again You recommend work with devotion. Now will You kindly tell me definitively which of the two is more beneficial?

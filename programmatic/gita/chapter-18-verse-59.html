Bhagavad Gita 18.59

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: यदहङ्कारमाश्रित्य न योत्स्य इति मन्यसे।मिथ्यैष व्यवसायस्ते प्रकृतिस्त्वां नियोक्ष्यति।।18.59।।

Meaning: If, in your self-conceit, you think, 'I will not fight,' your resolve is futile. Nature will compel you.

Bhagavad Gita 11.13

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: तत्रैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं प्रविभक्तमनेकधा। अपश्यद्देवदेवस्य शरीरे पाण्डवस्तदा।।11.13।।

Meaning: There, Arjuna beheld the whole universe, with its manifold divisions gathered together in one single spot within the body of the God of gods.

Bhagavad Gita 18.64

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: सर्वगुह्यतमं भूयः श्रृणु मे परमं वचः।इष्टोऽसि मे दृढमिति ततो वक्ष्यामि ते हितम्।।18.64।।

Meaning: Hear again My supreme word, the most secret of all; as I am exceedingly fond of you, I am telling you what is good for you.

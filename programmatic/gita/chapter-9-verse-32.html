Bhagavad Gita 9.32

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः। स्त्रियो वैश्यास्तथा शूद्रास्तेऽपि यान्ति परां गतिम्।।9.32।।

Meaning: By taking refuge in Me, even those of evil birth, women, Vaisyas, and Sudras, attain the supreme state.

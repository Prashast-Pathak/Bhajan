Bhagavad Gita 14.4

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: सर्वयोनिषु कौन्तेय मूर्तयः सम्भवन्ति याः।तासां ब्रह्म महद्योनिरहं बीजप्रदः पिता।।14.4।।

Meaning: Whatever forms are produced in any womb, O Arjuna, the Prakṛti is their great womb, and I am the sowing father.

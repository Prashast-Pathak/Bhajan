Bhagavad Gita 12.4

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: संनियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः।ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः।।12.4।।

Meaning: Having subdued all the senses, being even-minded, and engaged in the welfare of all beings, they too come to Me only.

Bhagavad Gita 18.77

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: तच्च संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य रूपमत्यद्भुतं हरेः। विस्मयो मे महान् राजन् हृष्यामि च पुनः पुनः।।18.77।।

Meaning: And, remembering over and over again that most marvelous form of Hari, great is my amazement, O King, and I rejoice again and again.

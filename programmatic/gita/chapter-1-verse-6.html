Bhagavad Gita 1.6

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: युधामन्युश्च विक्रान्त उत्तमौजाश्च वीर्यवान्। सौभद्रो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्व एव महारथाः॥

Meaning: There are the mighty Yudhamanyu, the very powerful Uttamauja, the son of Subhadra (Abhimanyu), and the sons of Draupadi. All of them are great chariot fighters (Maha-rathas).

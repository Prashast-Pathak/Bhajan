Bhagavad Gita 16.9

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः।प्रभवन्त्युग्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः।।16.9।।

Meaning: Holding this view, these men of lost souls and feeble understanding do cruel deeds, leading to the destruction of the world.

Bhagavad Gita 10.40

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: नान्तोऽस्ति मम दिव्यानां विभूतीनां परंतप। एष तूद्देशतः प्रोक्तो विभूतेर्विस्तरो मया।।10.40।।

Meaning: There is no limit to My divine manifestations. Here, I have briefly described the extent of such manifestations.

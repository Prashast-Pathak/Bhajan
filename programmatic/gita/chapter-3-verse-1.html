Bhagavad Gita 3.1

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: अर्जुन उवाच ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन। तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव॥

Meaning: Arjuna said: O Janardana, O Keshava, if You consider intelligence (knowledge) to be superior to fruitive action, then why do You urge me to engage in this ghastly warfare?

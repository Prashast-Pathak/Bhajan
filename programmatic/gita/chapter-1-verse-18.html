Bhagavad Gita 1.18

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: द्रुपदो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्वशः पृथिवीपते। सौभद्रश्च महाबाहुः शङ्खान्दध्मुः पृथक्पृथक्॥

Meaning: ...O King, Drupada, the sons of Draupadi, and the mighty-armed son of Subhadra (Abhimanyu)—all of them blew their respective conch shells.

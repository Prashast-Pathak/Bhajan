Bhagavad Gita 9.30

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाक्। साधुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः।।9.30।।

Meaning: If even the most sinful man worships Me with undivided devotion, he should be regarded as holy, for he has rightly resolved.

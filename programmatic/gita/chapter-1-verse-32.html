Bhagavad Gita 1.32

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: न काङ्क्षे विजयं कृष्ण न च राज्यं सुखानि च। किं नो राज्येन गोविन्द किं भोगैर्जीवितेन वा॥

Meaning: O Krishna, I do not desire victory, nor kingdom, nor happiness. O Govinda, of what use to us is a kingdom, happiness, or even life itself?

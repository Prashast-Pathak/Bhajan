Bhagavad Gita 1.3

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: पश्यैतां पाण्डुपुत्राणामाचार्य महतीं चमूम्। व्यूढां द्रुपदपुत्रेण तव शिष्येण धीमता॥

Meaning: O my teacher, behold the great army of the sons of Pandu, expertly arranged by your intelligent disciple, the son of Drupada.

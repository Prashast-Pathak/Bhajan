Bhagavad Gita 1.37

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: तस्मान्नार्हा वयं हन्तुं धार्तराष्ट्रान्स्वबान्धवान्। स्वजनं हि कथं हत्वा सुखिनः स्याम माधव॥

Meaning: Therefore, it is not proper for us to kill the sons of Dhritarashtra and our friends. What should we gain, O Madhava, and how could we be happy by killing our own kinsmen?

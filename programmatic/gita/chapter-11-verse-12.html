Bhagavad Gita 11.12

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: दिवि सूर्यसहस्रस्य भवेद्युगपदुत्थिता। यदि भाः सदृशी सा स्याद्भासस्तस्य महात्मनः।।11.12।।

Meaning: If a thousand suns were to rise at once in the sky, the resulting splendour would be like the splendour of that mighty One.

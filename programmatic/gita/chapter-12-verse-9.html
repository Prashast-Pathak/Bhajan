Bhagavad Gita 12.9

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम्।अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनञ्जय।।12.9।।

Meaning: If now you are unable to focus your mind on Me, then seek to reach Me, O Arjuna, through the practice of repetition.

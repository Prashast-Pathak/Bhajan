Bhagavad Gita 12.7

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात्।भवामि नचिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम्।।12.7।।

Meaning: Those whose minds are thus focused on Me, I soon become their savior from the ocean of mortal life.

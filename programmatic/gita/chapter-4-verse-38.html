Bhagavad Gita 4.38

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते। तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति॥

Meaning: In this world, there is nothing so sublime and pure as transcendental knowledge. Such knowledge is the mature fruit of all mysticism. And one who has become accomplished in the practice of yoga enjoys this knowledge within himself in due course of time.

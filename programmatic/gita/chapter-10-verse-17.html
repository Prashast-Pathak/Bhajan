Bhagavad Gita 10.17

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: कथं विद्यामहं योगिंस्त्वां सदा परिचिन्तयन्। केषु केषु च भावेषु चिन्त्योऽसि भगवन्मया।।10.17।।

Meaning: How can I, Your devotee, know You by constantly meditating on You? And in what ways, O Lord, should I meditate on You?

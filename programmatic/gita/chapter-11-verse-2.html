Bhagavad Gita 11.2

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: भवाप्ययौ हि भूतानां श्रुतौ विस्तरशो मया। त्वत्तः कमलपत्राक्ष माहात्म्यमपि चाव्ययम्।।11.2।।

Meaning: O Krsna, I have heard in detail about the origin and dissolution of all beings, which come from You, as well as Your immutable greatness.

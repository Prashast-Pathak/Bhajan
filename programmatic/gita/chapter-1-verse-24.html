Bhagavad Gita 1.24

Programmatic spiritual reference page

Sanskrit: सञ्जय उवाच एवमुक्तो हृषीकेशो गुडाकेशेन भारत। सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये स्थापयित्वा रथोत्तमम्॥

Meaning: Sanjaya said: O descendant of Bharata, having thus been addressed by Arjuna (Gudakesha), Lord Krishna (Hrishikesha) drew up the fine chariot in the midst of the armies of both parties.

About Chaturthi

The 4th day of each lunar fortnight dedicated to Lord Ganesha. Sankashti Chaturthi (Krishna Paksha) is especially powerful.

🌿
Fasting Guidance (Upvas Vidhi)
Fast Type
Until Moonrise
Intensity
🟡 Moderate
Fast until moonrise. Break fast after seeing the moon and offering modak.

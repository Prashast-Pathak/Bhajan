Purva Ashadha Nakshatra
Ruling Deity: Apas (Water Goddess)
🪔
Ruling Deity & Cosmic Role

The presiding deity of Purva Ashadha Nakshatra is Apas (Water Goddess). This divine force shapes the soul of every being born under this star, imparting their celestial qualities, cosmic duties, and spiritual gifts. By devotion to this deity through bhajan and mantra, native souls harmonise with their highest dharmic potential and unlock the blessings encoded in their birth star.

Ruling Planet Mantra — Shukra (Navagraha Stotra)
हिमकुन्दमृणालाभं दैत्यानां परमं गुरुम् ।
सर्वशास्त्रप्रवक्तारं भार्गवं प्रणमाम्यहम् ॥
Himakunda-mrnalabham daityanam paramam gurum |
Sarvashastra-pravaktaram Bhargavam pranamamy-aham ||
I bow to Shukra, white as snow and water lily, supreme teacher of asuras, propounder of all scriptures.
🕐 How to chant: Chant 108 times on the day of Shukra, facing East, ideally at sunrise or sunset.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Purva Ashadha Nakshatra?
Purva Ashadha is the 20th of 27 lunar mansions in Vedic Jyotish. It spans 13°20' of the zodiac and is ruled by Shukra with Apah (Varuna) as its presiding deity.
Which planet rules Purva Ashadha Nakshatra?
Purva Ashadha is ruled by Shukra in the Vimshottari Dasha system. The qualities of this planet significantly shape the nature and life patterns of natives born under this star.
Who is the deity of Purva Ashadha Nakshatra?
The presiding deity of Purva Ashadha is Apah (Varuna). Devotional worship and chanting associated with this deity may help align the native with the higher qualities encoded in their birth star.
What mantra is recommended for Purva Ashadha Nakshatra?
The Navagraha Stotra verse for Shukra is traditionally recommended. Chanting it 108 times on the day associated with Shukra may be spiritually beneficial.
On which day should Purva Ashadha natives worship?
The day of the week ruled by Shukra is considered auspicious for Purva Ashadha natives. Regular devotional practice on this day may help harmonise planetary energies.
How do I find my Janma Nakshatra?
Your Janma Nakshatra is determined by the Moon's position at the time of your birth. A free Kundali calculator at ournakshatra.com can calculate this instantly from your birth date, time, and place.
What bhajan is recommended for Purva Ashadha Nakshatra?
Bhajans and stotras aligned with Apah (Varuna) are traditionally recommended for Purva Ashadha natives. Devotional singing with sincerity is considered more important than the specific bhajan chosen.
Can my Nakshatra change?
No — your Janma Nakshatra is fixed at birth based on the Moon's position and does not change. However, the Moon transits through all 27 Nakshatras approximately every 27 days, affecting the day's quality.
